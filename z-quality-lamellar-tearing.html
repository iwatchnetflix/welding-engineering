Through-Thickness Properties and Lamellar Tearing

A Z designation verifies reduction of area in the through-thickness direction. It reduces material susceptibility but does not by itself prevent lamellar tearing; joint geometry, weld shrinkage and restraint remain controlling design variables.

Mechanism

Rolling can elongate non-metallic inclusions parallel to the plate surface. When weld contraction or service load imposes through-thickness strain, separation can develop between inclusion arrays. Lamellar tearing is typically a stepped crack in the parent material, approximately parallel to the rolled surface and commonly adjacent to highly restrained tee, corner or cruciform welds.

EN 10164 classes

EN 10164:2018 specifies improved deformation properties perpendicular to the product surface. The Z class is based on reduction of area in a through-thickness tensile test; it is neither a sulfur-content class nor a direct percentage probability of avoiding tearing.

ClassMinimum average reduction of areaMinimum individual result
Z1515%10%
Z2525%15%
Z3535%25%

For Z35, the specified average is 35% and the individual minimum is 25%; it is incorrect to require every result to reach 35% unless the purchase specification adds that condition. EN 10164 applies principally to fully killed steel products within its stated thickness and strength scope. Product eligibility must be checked before ordering.

Details with elevated susceptibility

  • Large single-sided fillet or partial-penetration welds deposited on the face of thick plate.
  • Tee, corner and cruciform joints that transmit weld shrinkage through the plate thickness.
  • Highly restrained nodes and details with excessive weld volume.
  • Poor fit-up that increases weld size or creates uneven shrinkage.
  • Attachments close to plate edges or regions with local segregation.

Design controls

The preferred control is to reduce or redirect through-thickness strain. Measures include reorienting the plate, changing the load path, reducing weld volume, balancing the welding sequence and using a joint detail that permits in-plane deformation. Buttering can alter the shrinkage path or reduce restraint, but the material, thickness and sequence require procedure qualification.

Z-quality and ultrasonic testing

Z-quality is specified when the design assessment identifies through-thickness strain demand that cannot be removed by detailing. Selection should follow the adopted design method, including the relevant EN 1993-1-10 provisions where applicable. Parent-material UT can identify laminations or loss of back-wall response in critical zones, but it does not measure through-thickness ductility and cannot replace a specified Z class.

GB/T 5313-2023 is the current Chinese product standard for steel plate with through-thickness properties. It provides the appropriate Chinese terminology—“厚度方向性能”和“断面收缩率”—but the purchase specification must still state the governing product standard and class rather than assume automatic equivalence with EN 10164.

Design and fabrication review

  1. Identify where weld shrinkage and service stress act through the product thickness.
  2. Assess restraint, weld volume, product thickness and consequence of cracking.
  3. Improve the joint detail before increasing the Z class.
  4. Select the required through-thickness class using the governing design method.
  5. Specify any parent-material UT zone, method and acceptance criterion before fabrication.
  6. Maintain material identity and plate orientation after cutting.
A Z class is evidence of through-thickness ductility; it is not a substitute for a sound joint detail.
