Weld NDT: Method, Testing Level and Acceptance Level

A weld-quality level, an NDT testing level and an NDT acceptance level are related, but they perform different functions. An inspection requirement is incomplete unless the governing application standard, examination method, testing level, acceptance standard, extent and timing are all stated.

Hierarchy of requirements

The contract and execution specification establish the applicable structural code, execution class or weld category, required weld quality and extent of examination. For European structural steelwork, EN 1090-2 provides execution requirements and refers to the relevant EN ISO welding and NDT standards. ISO 17635 gives general rules for selecting NDT methods, techniques and acceptance levels; it does not replace the project execution specification.

Quality level under ISO 5817

ISO 5817:2023 specifies quality levels B, C and D for imperfections in fusion-welded joints, with B representing the highest requirement on the finished weld. These are workmanship quality levels. ISO 5817 does not define an ultrasonic scan plan, radiographic technique or a universal fitness-for-service criterion. Metallurgical properties such as hardness and grain size are outside its scope.

A testing level defines the rigour of the examination technique, including coverage and sensitivity. An acceptance level sets limits for relevant indications. ISO 17635:2025 states that NDT acceptance levels cannot be interpreted as a direct, one-to-one translation of ISO 5817 quality levels for every indication.

Terminology in Chinese standards

The corresponding Chinese technical terms are “缺欠” (imperfection), “显示” (indication), “检测等级” (testing level) and “验收等级” (acceptance level). GB/T 34628-2017 is the current Chinese adoption of ISO 17635:2016, while ISO 17635 has since been revised in 2025. For ultrasonic testing, GB/T 11345-2023 is identical to ISO 17640:2018, and GB/T 29712-2023 is identical to ISO 11666:2018. The adopted edition must therefore be stated rather than assuming that every GB/T document is identical to the latest ISO edition.

Content of an inspection and test plan

Weld population → extent and selection → hold time → method → testing level → acceptance level → disposition → record

The instruction “10% UT to ISO 5817 B” is not technically complete. ISO 5817 is not a UT method standard, and the statement does not define the applicable UT technique, acceptance level, sample selection, hold time, expansion after rejection or re-examination after repair. Those items should be specified before fabrication begins.

Use of PAUT and mixed code systems

Phased-array ultrasonic testing is not accepted solely because it produces an encoded image. The written procedure must demonstrate coverage for the joint geometry and define probes, wedges, focal laws, reference sensitivity, calibration, data retention and evaluation. Where AWS D1.1 governs, its inspection and acceptance framework applies unless the contract formally establishes how an alternative ISO method and its results are to be used.

An NDT report should identify the weld, examination extent, method and procedure, testing level, acceptance criterion, personnel, equipment, result and disposition.
